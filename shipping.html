Important Notes

During peak periods such as November and December, shipping times may be extended due to high demand and carrier delays.

During the Easter holiday, delivery timelines may also be affected due to temporary closures of shipping partners.

All delivery times provided are estimates only and cannot be guaranteed.

A parcel is considered lost after 15 business days for EU & US orders and 21 business days for international orders.

If an order is returned due to an incorrect or incomplete address, the customer is responsible for all reshipping costs.

Cancellations or changes can be requested within 2 hours of placing an order during business hours. Once packaged or shipped, changes are no longer possible.
