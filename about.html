Where imagination takes the stage

Bubunany was created to give children a place to imagine, perform, and grow — away from screens and into storytelling.

Meet Julie

I’m Julie, a mom of two and a lifelong lover of acting. Years of performing taught me confidence, emotional intelligence, and the power of imaginative play.

I wanted my daughters — and all children — to experience that same joy and creativity.

Why Bubunany?

I saw how screens were taking over kids’ days, leaving less room for imagination, creativity, and play.

Bubunany is a beautifully simple puppet theater that turns any room into a stage for storytelling, solo adventures, or playdates.
